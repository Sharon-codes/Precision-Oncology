\section{Decision and scope}
\textbf{The strongest supportable revision is a new computational benchmark-construction audit.} It is not a validation of the supplied manuscript's headline binding predictor. The new paper contains executed public-data experiments, a proved and independently tested construction contract, trained metadata controls, explicit negative results, and quantified coverage limitations.

An initial panel of five AI reviewer roles examined methods, biology, editorial fit, executable source behavior and public-data construction. They exchanged objections and reviewed the revised draft. Their reports are included as a transparent simulated review record; none represents an external human referee or the journal. After correcting the issues below, the reviewers found the revised computations and bounded interpretations defensible. This is confidence in the demonstrated audit, not a prediction of acceptance.

\textbf{New title:} Batch-dependent negative sampling and marginal-preserving decoy construction in TCR--peptide benchmarks.

\textbf{Why the central story changed.} The supplied PDF and its pinned source repository do not establish the same experiment. The paper's claimed AUROC 0.8753 and 7,416 test examples could not be traced to archived predictions, checkpoints or cohort manifests. Archived source outputs instead contain AUROC 0.6810 and 4,820 examples. These discrepancies are unresolved; neither selecting the larger number nor inventing replacement training would fix them. The revised manuscript removes the original performance superiority claims and can stand on its newly executed evidence.

\section{Executed evidence and independent checks}
The principal dataset is the frozen VDJdb 2023-06-01 release. Human TRB/class-I, sequence and source-label syntax filters produce 3,182 distinct score-$\geq1$ positive tuples, 253 peptides and 41 source HLA strata. The paper distinguishes this raw-annotation cohort from nomenclature-filtered sensitivity populations.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Synthetic mechanism:} 4,096 rows, nine peptide categories, 100 sampler seeds. Fixed peptide-only AUROC is 0.7187 for sorted batches, 0.5246 for shuffled batches and 0.5000 for an exact cyclic control.
\item \textbf{Main source grid:} 541 runs and 3,770,361 generated decoys across confidence cohorts, input orders and batch sizes. Complete per-run results are supplied.
\item \textbf{Closed assignment:} 2,691/3,182 positives retained. Zero declared registry contradictions and exact retained marginal equality. Independent exhaustive verification examines 924,960 permutations across 166 fixtures.
\item \textbf{Rejection comparator:} 30 seeds on the exact common retained cohort. Contradictions are zero, but peptide TV and identity-oracle AUROC are approximately 0.135 and 0.599. Matching's 0.5 oracle follows from its contract, not model skill.
\item \textbf{Trained controls:} Five receptor-fold assignments and ten decoy seeds per source regime. The split-first peptide/HLA model gives sorted AUROC 0.5558, release-order 0.5168 and shuffled/matched 0.5000. The original construction-first sensitivity is retained.
\item \textbf{Distance separation:} Five independently generated component partitions have minimum cross-partition peptide edit distance 3. Feasible retained test coverage is 32.04--72.85\%; no unseen-peptide binding model was trained.
\item \textbf{HLA annotation robustness:} All 41 labels were checked against frozen official IPD-IMGT/HLA 3.52.0. Excluding A*08:01 and deleted legacy A*24:01 leaves 3,108 positives and 39 labels. Assignment retains 2,665 rows (85.75\%) across 21 strata. Source-sampler contradictions persist; independently regenerated split-first sorted metadata AUROC is 0.5619, versus 0.5 for shuffling and matching.
\item \textbf{Archived prediction verification:} A standalone program reconstructs stored aggregates and pooled/fold AUROC, average precision and Brier metrics from probability outputs, checking receptor-disjoint folds, common positive cohorts and label balance. The machine-readable report records exact assertion counts and verification scope.
\end{itemize}
The main text has five newly drawn vector figures; the supplement adds a batch-size figure and detailed tables. Original scientific images were not reused to imply that old model results were reproduced.
