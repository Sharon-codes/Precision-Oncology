# Independent computational immunology review

Reviewed 12 October 2026. Sources: supplied `Precision_Oncology(2).pdf` and repository snapshot declared by that manuscript, `0ba2d538065e2d3a8b4642547eb77447ecc89a5b`. This is an internal adversarial review, not an external journal decision. No underlying experimental binding data or model checkpoint was recovered by this reviewer.

## Recommendation and defensible scope

Do not submit the supplied manuscript. Its biological interpretation and central comparative performance claims exceed the recoverable evidence. A useful paper can be developed around an executable audit of this pipeline, with failures demonstrated on public sequences, but simulations of implementation defects alone cannot establish a new TCR binding predictor or broad failure of attention-based models. Current evidence supports **pipeline-specific validity checks**, not “pervasive” field-wide failure or an “unconfounded” clinically useful baseline. The direct-concatenation model could remain a candidate method only after genuinely independent training and evaluation.

## Critical biological findings

1. **The claimed human-only cohort is contradicted by the curation code.** `preprocess.py::process_vdjdb` filters `vdjdb.score == 3` and chain type, but does not filter the host species. McPAS and CEDAR do contain human filters. The common downstream QC never restores a human filter. Thus the code does not establish a human-only dataset. Figure 5's H-2 labels are biologically meaningful murine MHC alleles, not HLA supertypes. A real count requires the raw release and saved cohort; do not infer one from the figure.

2. **The declared sources and assays do not match the pipeline.** The manuscript says VDJdb, IEDB, and McPAS. `run()` merges VDJdb, McPAS, CEDAR, 10x Donor 1, and a T-PLL 10x sample. The last source calls positives by a hardcoded dextramer UMI cutoff of 10. This is an assay-specific classification, not automatically an experimentally resolved binding truth. Disease metadata mixes antigen species, source-protein inference, donor health status, and T-PLL patient status. These are not comparable labels and cannot support an interpretable pathology clustering claim. Source release, assay type, binding endpoint, host, donor/study and extraction rules need explicit provenance columns.

3. **The actual model is not demonstrably beta-only.** `cache_embeddings.py` caches alpha and beta CDR3 sequences, each to 30 tokens, plus peptide-HLA strings to 50 tokens. `TCRCollate` returns alpha, beta, and peptide-HLA embeddings. `unimodal_ablation.py` calls the model with all three and zeros both alpha and beta together. The original 110-token dimension corresponds to 30+30+50, not the manuscript's 95+15. A missing alpha sequence still has an embedding representation or lookup path. Missingness itself can identify source or cohort. Resolve this from the model and saved tensors, or abandon the beta-only claim.

4. **Allele resolution is partly invented.** `standardize_hla` turns serologic groups such as A2 or B27 into A*02:01 or B*27:01. A serologic group does not determine its two-field allele. It also describes its output as a “G-group”, but appending :01 does not establish G-group membership. Preserve ambiguous resolution, exclude it from allele-specific analyses, or represent it as uncertain. Never silently upgrade metadata precision.

5. **HLA sequence fallback can manufacture information and erase the real allele.** `dataset.py::get_hla_pseudo` substitutes a locus consensus for absent mappings and defaults to an A*02:01-like sequence for A/B and unrecognized cases. Mouse alleles can therefore be mapped to human fallback sequences. A class-II pseudo-sequence substitute lacks an allele-specific binding-groove definition. The mapping file, version, residue positions, availability flag, and exact mapping coverage must be audited. Unknown mapping should fail or be explicitly missing; it must not be portrayed as the actual allele's sequence.

6. **The input is an artificial concatenated sequence, not a structurally validated pMHC representation.** Caching concatenates peptide and HLA pseudo-sequence as a single ESM input. The language model was not here shown to understand this string as a physical peptide-MHC complex. Its token geometry is not “spatial thermodynamic variance”. The model can exploit allele motifs, padding, missingness, source and peptide length. If retained, call it a concatenated sequence representation and test separate peptide/HLA encodings as a sensitivity analysis.

7. **Known-positive exclusion is not implemented in the collator.** `TCRCollate` stores `positive_triplets_set` as `self.pos_set`, but never queries it. It checks peptide equality and index equality, then may draw a fallback peptide-HLA pair from a global pool. This does not exclude a TCR's known binding to another distinct peptide. There is no SHA256 known-positive hash trie in this sampler. The same issue appears in the comparator collator. One can therefore show code-level failure directly; the actual collision rate remains unknown without the data.

8. **Shuffled pairs are presumed negatives, not experimentally confirmed nonbinders.** Even a correct known-positive exclusion cannot eliminate unknown cross-reactivity. A beta chain also does not uniquely identify a complete alpha-beta receptor: the same beta sequence can occur with different alpha chains. Positive exclusion should operate at the actual input granularity; beta-only and paired-chain studies have different ambiguity. Keep positives, experimental negatives, and sampled unknown pairs distinct in all files and claims.

9. **Peptide-HLA swapping preserves the sampled ligand's allele but may create an easy receptor-allele shortcut.** The code moves peptide and HLA together, which is preferable to mismatching the peptide with an unrelated presentation allele. Yet the resulting cross-HLA pairs can become distinguishable through TCR allele-associated motifs rather than peptide recognition. A fair stress test includes HLA-matched negative sampling, source-matched sampling, fixed candidate sets, and allele-only/peptide-only/receptor-only models trained separately. Report performance within each peptide-HLA target and average across targets.

10. **The peptide-masked TCR-only AUC of 0.5 is an algebraic consequence of the evaluation design.** Every positive receptor input is duplicated once with label 0. After the ligand branch is zeroed, both rows produce identical scores. Thus this control must have AUROC 0.5 (up to tied-score conventions), regardless of receptor memorization or learned interaction. It cannot prove “cross-chain synergy” or absence of receptor bias. Train unimodal models separately and use independent fixed targets for substantive controls.

11. **The occlusion example is not a structural validation.** LPRRSGAAGA is an influenza nucleoprotein epitope associated with HLA-B*07:02, supported by IEDB assay records and primary influenza work. It is a 10-mer, so position 9 is not its C-terminal position. The claim that peaks at 2, 3 and 9 “correspond” to canonical anchors requires allele- and length-specific evidence. Peptide-MHC anchor sensitivity is different from TCR contact sensitivity. Zeroing a learned token also creates an off-distribution embedding. A valid biological test would compare per-residue substitution effects with held-out functional measurements or experimentally resolved contacts; current pictures should be removed from strong biological interpretation.

12. **Embedding projections do not validate immunogenetic relationships.** t-SNE/UMAP clusters can follow input HLA sequence, peptide identity, sequence length, missing alpha, species, or data source. HLA allele families are not equivalent to peptide-binding supertypes. Without registered quantitative controls and clean labels, describe any projection as exploratory visualization only. The source-mixing problem already makes the existing pathology figure uninterpretable.

13. **Balanced-decoy screening metrics cannot establish deployment utility.** On a 50% positive benchmark EF@100 cannot exceed 2; 1.98 means 99 of 100 dataset labels were positive in that artificial candidate mix. It says little about screening millions of pairs at a much lower binder prevalence. Report AUROC, target-macro AUPRC, sensitivity at a prespecified false-positive rate, and positive predictive value over stated hypothetical prevalences. Such prevalence calculations are mathematical projections, not measured clinical performance. Binding is also distinct from activation, antitumor efficacy, safety, and toxicity.

14. **Current comparator claims are not biologically or technically fair.** The repository comparator script defines local simplified NetTCR/TITAN classes. The main candidate gets alpha/beta/HLA inputs, whereas these comparators receive beta and peptide. TITAN's published method includes transfer learning/SMILES configurations absent in the visible proxy. The native high AUROC figures do not prove exact implementation equivalence. Label proxies accurately and avoid stating the published methods fail. Separate matched-information comparisons from each method's complete native input comparison.

## Reviewer debate and decision criteria

I concur with the methods reviewer that no theoretical statement makes OOD logistic regression or convolutional outputs necessarily constant. A constant-score result is a warning about training, saturation, unknown-token encoding, loading, or evaluation. It is not biological evidence against the method class. I concur with the editor that simulated counterexamples are useful but insufficient by themselves to promise a strong ISCLS submission given the existing benchmark literature.

Minimum stronger study: one real public dataset with a preserved source manifest; exact species/chain/allele resolution audit; immutable distance-separated positive groups; negatives generated within each split under documented constraints; independent matched-information baseline training; repeated fixed splits; target-macro and group-bootstrap statistics; experimentally validated negative or external data if available. All original numerical superiority and clinical claims must remain unverified until traceable predictions are recovered or rerun. The strongest honest deliverable now can be a polished, executable audit manuscript with an explicit empirical-validation boundary, not a cosmetically improved high-AUC claim.
