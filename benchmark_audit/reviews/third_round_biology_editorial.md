# Independent third-round biological and editorial review

Reviewed 12 October 2026. This assessment began from `paper/manuscript.tex`, `paper/supplementary.tex`, their compiled PDFs, the declared cohort definition and primary literature. I did not use prior referee verdicts as a starting point. I subsequently compared the reviewer-source-access concern with the fresh reproducibility referee. A follow-up source review on the same date verified the final literature, source-access and HLA-caption edits described below. The parent agent is recompiling the updated PDFs; this follow-up does not claim that the newly compiled layout has already been inspected.

## Verdict and confidence boundary

The revised paper supports a **narrow computational construction audit**, not a biological binding-prediction study. I found no remaining biological contradiction that invalidates its central, bounded claims. The interpretation is substantially more defensible than a claim of neural-model superiority would be: registry contradictions, empirical marginal balance, feasibility and biological nonbinding are distinguished throughout.

The targeted literature revision and a concrete, prepared reviewer-access route have now been verified in the revised sources. **I have no unresolved biological/editorial source-content blocker for the bounded audit.** Actual provision of the evidence bundle remains a submission-handling task for the human authors; neither the manuscript nor this review asserts that the editor has received or approved that route. Confidence is high that the present biological terminology and principal cohort counts are internally coherent; moderate that the work provides a sufficiently distinct methods contribution for external review. Journal relevance is plausible, but editorial judgments about breadth and significance remain uncertain. This is not an acceptance prediction or guarantee.

## Third-round conditions and verified disposition

1. **Cite the closest frequency-bias precedent, TEINet.** Jiang, Huo and Li (2023) explicitly contrast uniform epitope-category sampling with sampling according to positive epitope frequencies, explain the resulting epitope-only shortcut, and report inflated performance under the former strategy. This is closer to the central frequency mechanism than a generic citation to negative-repertoire bias. The manuscript should distinguish its executed *within-batch permutation to unique-ligand fallback* mechanism and maximum closed-subset contract from the established general observation that epitope frequencies can encode construction labels. A faithful TEINet neural replication is unnecessary for the current claim. The independent within-HLA rejection comparator must continue to be described as a controlled reference, not an official TEINet implementation.

   **Closed in revised source.** The Introduction now explicitly describes TEINet's uniform-category versus positive-frequency-weighted comparison and cites `Jiang2023`; the Discussion also credits it. The verified bibliography has the correct authors, journal, volume, issue, article number and DOI. The following paragraph limits this paper's contribution to the executed batch fallback and later contract. The rejection comparator remains clearly separate from an official published model.

2. **Credit earlier known-partner exclusion and partition-first construction.** Moris et al. (2021) explicitly exclude known peptide partners during shuffled-negative generation and discuss inability to generate shuffled negatives in folds dominated by one epitope. EPIC-TRACE (2023), already cited for its model architecture, states in section 2.3 that negatives are generated separately for training/validation and test folds. Cite these at the relevant recommendations. The new result is the measured complementary-fold effect and explicit assignment feasibility, not the invention of splitting before negative generation.

   **Closed in revised source.** The Introduction now cites `Moris2021,Korpela2023` for these established procedures. The practical-recommendations paragraph specifically starts from earlier partition-first generation and cites Korpela. The Moris bibliographic entry matches the verified primary metadata. The contribution paragraph remains bounded and consistent with this attribution.

3. **Make executable audit evidence available to reviewers.** Main Methods places the exact implementation in the “author reproducibility archive”; the supplement says provenance/license information is in a separate author archive; package instructions warn against providing that archive wholesale to anonymous reviewers. Hashes and a prose description alone cannot substantiate an “unmodified AST” execution claim. Supply a lawful reviewer-access route to the exact pinned source and executable audit, with source notices preserved, or arrange controlled access through the editor. A standalone equivalent sampler helps interpret the contract but cannot by itself establish what the audited original source executed. This is a practical submission-readiness condition, not a biological reanalysis request.

   **Closed for the prepared deliverable; execution remains part of actual submission.** Main Supplementary Information and Data and Code Availability now identify the complete, non-anonymous source-evidence archive, preservation of copyright/provenance, and the proposed editor-mediated designated-reviewer route. Supplementary Methods describes the same exact pinned implementation and executable audit. `REPRODUCIBILITY_README.md` explicitly tells authors to provide the bundle to the editor and states that no external submission or correspondence has occurred. This removes the ambiguous instruction that reviewers have no route to the central source evidence. It does not establish that the journal has accepted an access arrangement; human authors must make the bundle available when submitting.

## Supported biological interpretation

- **Target key.** The declared tuple is exact CDR3-beta, peptide and source HLA label. The main text explicitly explains that omission of alpha-chain, V/J and full receptor context prevents interpreting a registry contradiction as a measured false-negative rate for complete receptors. This is the appropriate distinction. For maximal precision, “held-out CDR3-beta groups” is preferable wherever the shorter “unseen receptors” might be read as unseen complete alpha-beta receptors; the current Methods already defines the grouping.
- **Decoy semantics.** Unknown pairs are explicitly of unknown binding status. Equal marginals do not establish valid nonbinding, molecular interaction learning or removal of all shortcuts. These limits appear in the abstract, Results, Discussion and Conclusions, not solely in an appendix. The source comparator also moves HLA with the donor; the text correctly admits that source-versus-assignment comparisons change several constraints together.
- **Registry scope.** Including all eligible scores in the exclusion registry is defensible for an avoidable-contradiction audit because confidence is described as database evidence, not calibrated truth. The narrower-reference sensitivity makes the dependence transparent. A prospective binding evaluation would require a different ascertainment design, but this manuscript does not claim one.
- **HLA nomenclature.** I independently parsed the retained frozen nomenclature file using a separate short script. Exactly HLA-A*08:01 and HLA-A*24:01 lacked active two-field-prefix matches. The deleted-allele file contains the documented historical A*2401 deletion record, and both checksums agree with `official_annotation_validation.json`. The manuscript correctly avoids silent recoding and distinguishes source strata from genuine alleles. Reporting the original frozen source population and a full exclusion sensitivity is defensible for this audit; it must not be presented as 41 validated biological alleles.
- **Cohort arithmetic.** Independently applying the stated raw-data filters and tuple deduplication reproduced 3,182 primary tuples, 3,061 CDR3-beta sequences, 253 peptides and 41 source HLA strata. Confidence filtering, exact two-field syntax and canonical-sequence eligibility deliberately define a restricted database subset; the paper makes no population prevalence claim.
- **Coverage.** Retained HLA and peptide counts, lost strata, distance-separated retention, duplicate negative tuples and alternative optimal cycles are explicitly described. The paper does not use “maximum” to imply preservation of the entire original biological population. This is essential because the retained population is a selected feasible subset.
- **Clinical interpretation.** No clinical specificity, affinity, patient outcome, prospective precision or improved recognition prediction is claimed. The balanced decoy task is also distinguished from clinical prevalence in the supplement. No new clinical experiment is required merely to sustain the narrower computational claims.
