# Journal decision — 12 October 2026

Final target: **Network Modeling Analysis in Health Informatics and Bioinformatics** (Springer Nature), following the author's final preference to retain Springer.

Three independent AI journal-fit agents considered computational scope, immunological relevance and skeptical editorial screening. After reconsidering CBC's biological-insight restriction, all three judged NetMAHIB the closer match to this manuscript's methodological contribution. The work audits computational benchmark construction and quantifies a constrained correction; it does not demonstrate a superior biological binding predictor.

The official scope includes graph theory, statistics, linear algebra and machine learning for bioinformatics. A relevant precedent is Awan et al. (2021), “Benchmarking mass spectrometry based proteomics algorithms using a simulated database”, DOI: 10.1007/s13721-021-00298-3. The journal is hybrid and the subscription route carries no APC. General applicability beyond the audited implementation remains the main editorial question; acceptance and rapid review are not guaranteed.
